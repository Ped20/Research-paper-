† The osmotic potentials for study 1 are **as reported by the authors**. Applying the Michel and Kaufmann (1973) relation at 25 °C gives −0.05, −0.15 and −0.30 MPa for 5%, 10% and 15% PEG-6000 respectively (Figure 2), substantially less negative. The discrepancy is several-fold and affects every downstream comparison. **Trace these values to the primary source before the table is used quantitatively.** Study 10, by contrast, prepared its solutions directly from the equation and its stated potentials are internally consistent.

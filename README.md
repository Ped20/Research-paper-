# Research-paper-

Structural blueprints for writing research papers, packaged as agent-loadable skills.

## What's here

### `skills/writing-plant-science-papers/`

A paragraph-level structural blueprint for **molecular biology, plant science, and agricultural research papers** — the biology counterpart to a systems-conference writing guide.

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `SKILL.md` | The bluebook. Evidence ladder, section blueprints, word budgets, venue table, style and statistics rules, workflow, self-check |
| `references/manuscript-scaffold.md` | Fillable section-by-section scaffold with slots for every required element |
| `references/figure-table-plan.md` | Figure/table architecture, panel craft, legend templates, section↔figure mapping |
| `references/reporting-checklists.md` | Hard submission gates: MIQE, MIAPPE, MINSEQE, statistics, integrity |

## How this differs from a systems-paper blueprint

A systems paper's currency is architectural novelty; a biology paper's currency is **evidence strength for a causal claim**. The blueprint is built around that difference:

- **The Evidence Ladder** (L0 association → L7 field impact) with a *verb ceiling*: you may not write "regulates" on the strength of a correlation.
- **Figure-first planning** — the display items *are* the argument, and they are planned before any prose is written.
- **Alternatives and limitations** as mandatory Discussion paragraphs, replacing the systems "discuss alternatives for every design choice" rule.
- **Field-trial and multi-environment obligations** that have no systems-paper analogue: statistical → agronomic → economic significance.
- **Reporting standards** (MIQE, MIAPPE, MINSEQE) as gates rather than afterthoughts.

## Integrity note

These documents provide **structural guidance only**. They do not generate findings, figures, images, or citations, and nothing produced with them should be submitted unread. Fabrication, image manipulation, and memory-generated citations are explicitly prohibited — see `SKILL.md` §12.
